Create a premium, modern, professional portfolio website for a Salesforce Developer.

## IMPORTANT — RESUME AS THE PRIMARY DATA SOURCE

The user will upload a resume along with this request.

Before generating the website:

1. Read and analyze the entire uploaded resume.
2. Extract all relevant information from the resume.
3. Use the extracted resume information to populate the portfolio automatically.
4. Do NOT ask the user to manually enter information that is already available in the resume.
5. Do NOT invent, assume, or fabricate any information.
6. If a section does not have enough information in the resume, either omit it or display a suitable empty state.
7. Preserve the factual accuracy of:

   * Name
   * Job titles
   * Companies
   * Employment dates
   * Education
   * Certifications
   * Skills
   * Projects
   * Responsibilities
   * Achievements
   * Links
   * Contact information
8. Do not exaggerate responsibilities, experience, technical expertise, or achievements.
9. Where the resume contains inconsistent or duplicate information, use the most recent/current information where it is clearly identifiable.
10. Keep the website content professional and recruiter-friendly.

The uploaded resume is the **single source of truth** for personal, professional, educational, project, and certification information.

Build a professional Salesforce Developer portfolio designed for:
